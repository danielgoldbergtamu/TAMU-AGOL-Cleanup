What the statuses mean

0 · kept
The person is current: Entra places them in a current-affiliation group (enrolled student, employee, faculty, instructor), or their account has an override. Nothing happens to the account.
1 · flagged
The person is no longer current: not found in Entra, or found with no current affiliation (for example a former student). The flag date starts a 30-day notice period, in which the owner, and their supervisor if one is on record, are to be told to collect their content. The pipeline does not send these notices yet.
2 · listed for removal
Flagged for 30 days or more without becoming current again, or an account that owns nothing. Being listed is not removal: an account or its content is removed only after the maintainer has reviewed and approved that specific set, and content only after a verified backup.

Every run re-checks every member: anyone found current again goes back to 0, whatever their status. Projected status is what this run's dry run would set; the stored status changes only on a real run, which has not been run yet.

Feature storage, now and after each group

Removal groups

Empty accounts need no backup (nothing to keep, and an SSO account is recreated at its next login). Status 2 content waits for the verified backup. Status 1 reaches status 2 after 30 days. Administrators and non-standard roles are listed for review and never counted as removable.

Select layers for deletion by period

For the year-by-year Lifeboat backups: choose a period and tiers, select the layers, and download them as a CSV for the delete script. The CSV is a proposal, not an approval. Nothing is deleted until the maintainer has reviewed that list and approved it in writing, and only for years with a verified backup. Selections stay in this browser until you download them; the site never deletes anything.

Accounts that own nothing

Group 1 (proposed for removal without a backup) is only the empty accounts of people who have left, with a NetID login and no groups owned (rule of 20 October 2026). Current people's empty accounts are kept; empty ArcGIS-login accounts and group owners take the normal 30-day flag. Cautions: an ArcGIS login account is not recreated at the next sign-in, the way a NetID account is; an account that owns groups cannot be deleted until the groups are reassigned; current members and anyone who signed in within 90 days are listed by the empty-account rule regardless of affiliation. Accounts with no caution are the cleanest first set.

Multi-Account Holders

Each account belongs to the person whose NetID it matched in Entra: an ArcGIS login with any username (a class or project account, for example) belongs to the person on its email. A person's accounts are assessed together: if the person is current, all their accounts are kept; if they have left, their flagged accounts reach status 2 together.

Current accounts among them

Accounts that are current themselves (found in Entra with a current affiliation). An account kept only because another account of the same person is current is not counted here.
