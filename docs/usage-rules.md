# ArcGIS Online usage rules and guidelines

The text Texas A&M users are pointed at (written February 2026). Adapt it for your institution.
The rules are deliberately few and short, because every one of them is quoted back when content is
removed.

## Rules

1. ArcGIS Online (AGOL) is a tool solely for managing and sharing **short-term** GIS projects
   across Texas A&M. **It is not a long-term storage and sharing solution** for important GIS
   projects.
2. AGOL and all applications, data layers, and items that are published to it are **owned and
   managed by the Texas A&M GIS Help Desk**.
3. It is the **user's responsibility** to save their data on another platform (personal disk, hard
   drive, etc.) should they need to access data beyond their tenure at Texas A&M.

## Guidelines

1. Avoid uploading large files and GIS layers wherever possible.
2. Remove all items from AGOL whenever they no longer need to be accessed.
3. Be mindful of data storage formats (vector against raster) and how they affect file size.

## Something users cannot do for themselves

An ordinary AGOL user cannot see how large their own items are. Only an administrator's report
shows item sizes. A per-user quota is therefore something users cannot check against on their own.
Give them a way to see their usage before holding them to a number.
